Founded with care

A calm, welcoming practice for healing, growth, and connection

Positive Inner Self was created to be a safe, supportive place where people can heal, grow, and reconnect with themselves. We believe care should feel human, respectful, and aligned with each client's story, values, and goals.

Our practice serves children, teens, adults, couples, and families. We take a collaborative, client-centered approach and offer both counseling and wellness services to support emotional and physical well-being.

🏡

Our Mission

We exist to provide compassionate, accessible mental health and wellness services that honor the whole person. Every interaction is an opportunity to create safety, foster connection, and support lasting change.

Our Story

Positive Inner Self began with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to care that feels genuinely supportive. What started as a small counseling practice has grown into a team of dedicated providers offering a range of services — from individual and family therapy to wellness treatments like massage and acupuncture.

Located in Beaverton, Oregon, we've built a practice that reflects the community we serve. Our team brings diverse backgrounds and specialties, united by a shared commitment to client-centered care.

We continue to grow and evolve, always guided by our core values and the feedback of those we serve.
