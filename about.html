How we got here

Our Story

Positive Inner Self began with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to care that feels genuinely supportive. What started as a small counseling practice has grown into a team of dedicated providers offering a range of services — from individual and family therapy to wellness treatments like massage and acupuncture.

Located in Beaverton, Oregon, we've built a practice that reflects the community we serve. Our team brings diverse backgrounds and specialties, united by a shared commitment to client-centered care.

We continue to grow and evolve, always guided by our core values and the feedback of those we serve.
