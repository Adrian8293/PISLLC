Care that honors the whole person

Positive Inner Self was created to be a safe, supportive place where people can heal, grow, and reconnect with themselves. We believe care should feel human, respectful, and aligned with each client's story, values, and goals.

Our practice serves children, teens, adults, couples, and families. We take a collaborative, client-centered approach and offer both counseling and wellness services to support emotional and physical well-being.
