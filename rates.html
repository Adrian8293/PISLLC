Insurance & Payment

We accept many insurance plans and can help clients understand their benefits before getting started. We're in-network with several major insurance providers. Coverage varies by plan, so we recommend verifying your specific benefits before your first appointment.

We can provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement if your insurance offers that option.

Common questions

Common Questions

How do I verify my insurance benefits?
Contact us with your insurance information and we'll help you understand your coverage. You can also call the member services number on your insurance card and ask about mental health benefits.
What if I don't have insurance?
We offer private pay options at the rates listed above. We're happy to discuss payment plans or options that might work for your situation.
Do you offer sliding scale fees?
Availability varies by provider. Please reach out to discuss your situation and we'll do our best to find a solution that works for you.
What is your cancellation policy?
We ask for 24-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to reschedule.
